Our Story

Rooted in
Guatemalan Pride

Guzman Outdoors was built on one belief: a well-kept property is a point of pride — and every customer deserves the same care we'd give our own home. What started as a one-truck operation in Williamston has grown into a trusted name across mid-Michigan, built entirely on results you can see from the street.

Our roots run deep in Guatemalan heritage — a culture that values hard work, beautiful craftsmanship, and showing up with everything you've got. Those values show in every clean edge, every precise cut, and every lawn we leave better than we found it.

"We treat every lawn like it's the front of our own home."

— The Guzman Family
