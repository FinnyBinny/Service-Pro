Our Story

Rooted in
Guatemalan Pride

Guzman Landscaping was founded on one simple belief: a well-kept property is a point of pride — and every customer deserves the same care we'd give our own home. What started as a one-truck operation has grown into a trusted name across the region, built entirely on word of mouth and results you can see from the street.

Our roots run deep in Guatemalan heritage — a culture that values hard work, beautiful craftsmanship, and showing up with everything you've got. Those values aren't just words to us. They're in every clean edge, every precise trim, and every lawn we leave better than we found it.
